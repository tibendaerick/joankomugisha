Her story

Daughter of Brig. Gen. Tarkiwa Francis M. and the late Capt. Joy Mweteise, Hon. Komugisha Joan has spent over a decade mobilizing communities against poverty including youth, women and families.

Chairperson

Greater Mubende Women Association. Over 30,000 women across five districts.

Director

Joy Vocational Center. Free skills for youth and women in Mubende Municipality.

Chairperson

Children of Soldiers Association. Education and mentorship in the barracks.

PLU Mobilizer

Greater Mubende. Teaching the PLU pillars at all levels including ghettos, women etcetera.

Head Patron

International Consensus Center. Socio-economic development and collaboration.

Travel Consultant

Degree in Tourism and Operations Management, Nkumba University.

Speaker

Tukazane Ghetto Structure. Over 15 ghetto communities in Mubende Municipality

What she is building

  • Ghetto youth upliftment. 640 ghetto youths mobilized to engage in government programmes e.g PDM, Emyooga.
  • Women's economic empowerment. Financial literacy and income-generating work for women across Uganda.
  • Community sensitization. Fighting corruption, protecting security, working productively.
  • Revival of military families. Education and skills for soldiers' children.
